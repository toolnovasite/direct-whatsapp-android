Privacy Policy — Direct Chat for Android

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Direct Chat for Android (“Direct Chat”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a utility that helps you open a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business chat without first saving a phone number as a contact. Developer: Abdul Sami. Privacy contact: shining.8090@gmail.com.

Information you enter and use on your device

  • Phone numbers and shared text: The app processes the phone number you enter or share into Direct. If you share a block of text, Direct looks for a phone number to prefill the phone field; it does not upload the shared text to the developer. The current app does not compose or send message text. When you choose WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, the app opens a chat link containing the phone number. The selected third-party app then processes data under its own policies. Direct does not send messages automatically.
  • Call logs: Direct Chat does not read, collect, or store your phone call-log entries. You can enter a number manually, paste a number when you tap Paste, or share a number into the app from another app.
  • Saved recent numbers: This optional feature is off by default. If enabled, up to 30 recent numbers are saved in the app’s local Android preferences on your device. Turn the switch off to remove this saved list. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data under normal Android behavior.
  • Clipboard: The app reads clipboard text only after you tap Paste, to find a phone number.
  • Shared links: If you intentionally share a phone number or text to Direct, the app may read that incoming text to prefill the number field.

Advertising and third-party SDKs

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and display banner advertisements. When ads load, Google and its advertising technology may automatically receive or process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and ad-related events. Google may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's documentation and policies. These requests are transmitted to Google; they are not the app's local phone-number history. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

The app does not operate its own account system or developer-hosted server for phone numbers or saved recent numbers. Advertising SDK data handling is governed in part by Google's services and settings.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request advertisements. Opening a WhatsApp chat is handed off to the selected external app or Android handler.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the advertising SDK to check network connectivity.

Data retention and deletion

Numbers are not saved by the app unless you enable Save recent numbers. Turning that option off deletes the app's saved list. The app does not access phone call-log entries. Advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to Google's policies and settings. To ask a privacy question, email shining.8090@gmail.com.

Security

Local app preferences are stored by Android in the app's private storage. Network requests for advertising are handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK using Google's infrastructure. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

Children

Direct is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children. The app does not ask users to create accounts or submit personal profile details to the developer. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device and advertising data as described above. The developer must configure the app's target audience, ad requests, and Play Console declarations accurately for the intended audience and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when appropriate. The version published at this URL is the current policy.
