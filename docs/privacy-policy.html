Privacy Policy — Direct for Android

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Direct for Android (“Direct”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a utility that helps you open a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business chat without first saving a phone number as a contact. Developer: Abdul Sami. Privacy contact: shining.8090@gmail.com.

Information you enter and use on your device

  • Phone numbers and draft messages: The app processes the number you enter and any text you share into the app to prepare a click-to-chat link. It does not send a message automatically. When you choose WhatsApp, the number and any message text included in the link are handed to the selected WhatsApp app or Android/browser. WhatsApp/Meta and any selected app then process data under their own policies.
  • Recent calls: If you choose “Recent calls” and grant Android’s call-log permission, the app reads recent call entries on your device so you can select a number. The app does not upload your call log to the developer. You can deny permission and continue to use the main number-entry feature.
  • Saved recent numbers: This optional feature is off by default. If enabled, up to 30 recent numbers are saved in the app’s local Android preferences on your device. Turn the switch off to remove this saved list. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data under normal Android behavior.
  • Clipboard: The app reads clipboard text only after you tap Paste, to find a phone number.
  • Shared links: If you intentionally share a phone number or text to Direct, the app may read that incoming text to prefill the number field.

Advertising and third-party SDKs

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and display banner advertisements. When ads load, Google and its advertising technology may automatically receive or process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and ad-related events. Google may use this information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's documentation and policies. These requests are transmitted to Google; they are not the app's local phone-number history. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

The app does not operate its own account system or developer-hosted server for phone numbers, call logs, or saved recent numbers. Advertising SDK data handling is governed in part by Google's services and settings.

Permissions

  • READ_CALL_LOG: Used only when you request the Recent calls picker.
  • INTERNET: Required to request advertisements and open external web links.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the advertising SDK to check network connectivity.

Data retention and deletion

Numbers are not saved by the app unless you enable Save recent numbers. Turning that option off deletes the app's saved list. Recent call entries are read for display when requested and are not retained by the app. Advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to Google's policies and settings. To ask a privacy question, email shining.8090@gmail.com.

Security

Local app preferences are stored by Android in the app's private storage. Network requests for advertising are handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK using Google's infrastructure. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

Children

Direct is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising may be delivered by Google according to the app's configured audience and applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when appropriate. The version published at this URL is the current policy.
